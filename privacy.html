Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

1. What OneLet Does

OneLet is a word puzzle game. You change one letter at a time to turn one word into another. Every puzzle is generated in advance and shipped inside the app, so the game works with no internet connection at all — apart from the advertising and purchase functions described below, which only run when you choose to use them.

2. Information We Collect

We collect nothing. We have no server, no account system and no login. The app never sends your progress, your words, or anything else to us. We cannot see how you play, and we could not identify you if we tried.

The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and no social-media SDK. It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

This is not the whole story, and the next section is the reason. OneLet shows optional rewarded video ads supplied by Google. Google collects data through those ads. We do not receive that data, but it would be dishonest to describe this app as collecting nothing without saying so.

3. Advertising

OneLet includes optional rewarded video advertising, provided by Google AdMob. Two things are true of every ad in this game:

  • You choose to watch. Ads never interrupt a puzzle, never appear between levels, and never block your progress. The only way to see one is to tap a button that offers a reward in exchange.
  • Nothing in the game is locked behind an ad. Every level is completable without watching anything.

To serve those ads, Google's advertising software collects information from your device. Depending on your settings this can include advertising identifiers, your IP address, device and operating-system details, and interaction data such as whether an ad was viewed or completed. This processing is carried out by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's own policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

On iOS, before any tracking takes place, the system asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, you can still play the entire game and still watch rewarded ads — the ads are simply not personalised. Declining costs you nothing in this app.

If you would rather have no advertising at all, the app offers a one-time purchase that removes it. See section 5.

4. What Is Stored on Your Device

The app saves a small amount of data locally, using the operating system's standard app-storage, so it can remember where you are between sessions:

  • your progress — which levels you have finished and how many stars each one earned;
  • your light — the in-game currency, and which hint items you own;
  • your streak — how many consecutive days you have solved the daily puzzle, and how many streak shields you hold;
  • your notebook — the words you have passed through while climbing;
  • your settings — sound and vibration on or off;
  • a few small flags — whether the tutorial has been shown, whether the rating prompt has already been offered, whether a permission request has been answered, and whether the ad-free purchase is active on this device.

This is a few kilobytes of text. It stays in the app's private storage area on your device. We cannot read it, retrieve it, or recover it for you. It is not backed up to any server of ours, because we do not have one.

5. Purchases

OneLet offers a single one-time purchase that removes advertising. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never see your name, your card details or your billing address. The app only stores a flag saying whether the purchase is active on this device.

If you reinstall the app or change device, use Restore Purchases in Settings. You will not be asked to pay twice.

Buying the ad-free version does not remove the optional rewarded videos — those are a trade you choose to make, and taking them away would punish you for paying.

6. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app can remind you about the daily puzzle. These are local notifications, scheduled by your own device. There is no push server, so no message about you ever leaves your phone. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings.

7. Children

OneLet is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app shows advertising supplied by Google, we request that ads be treated as directed to a general audience and served without personalisation where required by law. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question about this, please write to us.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

We hold no data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Everything the app stores lives on your device, and you control it completely:

  • Settings → Delete my data erases your progress, light, streak, notebook and settings immediately. The ad-free purchase record is kept, so that paying once is enough;
  • deleting the app removes everything, including the purchase record — which you can bring back with Restore Purchases.

To ask Google to delete data collected through advertising, use the links in section 3; that data is held by Google, not by us.

9. Your Rights

Data-protection law gives you rights of access, correction, deletion and objection. These rights are exercised against whoever holds your data. We hold none, so there is nothing to request from us. For data collected by Google's advertising services, contact Google using the links in section 3.

10. Changes to This Policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, we update this page and change the date at the top. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy, or anything else: yebappcontact@gmail.com. We read every message.
