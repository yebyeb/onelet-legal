Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

1. What OneLet Does

OneLet is a word puzzle game. You change one letter at a time to turn one word into another. Every puzzle is generated in advance and shipped inside the app, so the game itself works with no internet connection. The only parts that use the network are advertising, purchases, and the Apple services described below (iCloud and Game Center).

2. Information We Collect

We collect nothing. We have no server, no account system and no login. The app never sends your progress, your words, or anything else to us. We cannot see how you play, and we could not identify you if we tried.

The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and no social-media SDK. It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

This is not the whole story, and the next section is the reason. OneLet shows advertising supplied by Google. Google collects data through those ads. We do not receive that data, but it would be dishonest to describe this app as collecting nothing without saying so.

3. Advertising

OneLet is free to play and is supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob. There are three kinds of ad:

  • Rewarded videos — always your choice. You only see one by tapping a button that offers a reward (for example gold or a tool) in exchange. Nothing in the game is locked behind one.
  • A banner at the bottom of the game screen, from level 6.
  • Interstitial ads between levels, from level 8 — at first once every few levels. They never interrupt a puzzle while you are playing it.

The first five levels show no advertising at all.

To serve those ads, Google's advertising software collects information from your device. Depending on your settings this can include advertising identifiers, your IP address, device and operating-system details, and interaction data such as whether an ad was viewed or completed. This processing is carried out by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's own policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

On iOS, before any tracking takes place, the system asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, you can still play the entire game and still watch rewarded ads — the ads are simply not personalised. Declining costs you nothing in this app.

If you would rather not see banner and interstitial ads, the app offers a one-time purchase that removes them. See section 6.

4. What Is Stored on Your Device

The app saves a small amount of data locally, using the operating system's standard app storage, so it can remember where you are between sessions:

  • your progress — which levels you have finished and how many stars each one earned, and whether today's daily puzzle is solved;
  • your gold — the in-game currency — and the tools you own;
  • your notebook — the words you have passed through while climbing;
  • your settings — language, sound and vibration;
  • a few small flags — whether the tutorial has been shown, whether a permission request has been answered, whether today's wheel spin or gift box has been used, and which purchases are active on this device.

This is a few kilobytes of text. We cannot read it, retrieve it, or recover it for you.

5. Apple Services: iCloud and Game Center

iCloud. If you are signed in to iCloud, the app keeps a copy of the data in section 4 in your own iCloud account, so your progress follows you to a new device. This copy is stored by Apple under your Apple ID; we have no access to it. You can turn it off in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud.

Game Center. If you use Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center under your Game Center profile and may be visible to other players according to your Game Center settings. This is Apple's service and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Purchases

Optional purchases are available: gold packs, a one-time starter pack, a one-time purchase that removes banner and interstitial ads, and the Summit Pass subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never see your name, your card details or your billing address. The app only stores which purchases are active.

If you reinstall the app or change device, use Restore Purchases in Settings. You will not be asked to pay twice.

The ad-removal purchase does not remove the optional rewarded videos — those are a trade you choose to make, and taking them away would punish you for paying.

7. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app can remind you once a day that a new daily puzzle is ready. These are local notifications, scheduled by your own device during daytime hours only. There is no push server, so no message about you ever leaves your phone. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings.

8. Children

OneLet is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app shows advertising supplied by Google, we request that ads be treated as directed to a general audience and served without personalisation where required by law. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question about this, please write to us.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

We hold no data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Everything the app stores lives on your device and in your own iCloud account, and you control it completely:

  • Settings → Delete my data erases your progress, gold, tools, notebook and settings on this device and in your iCloud copy. Your language and purchase records are kept, so that paying once is enough;
  • deleting the app removes the data on this device; purchases can be brought back with Restore Purchases.

To ask Google to delete data collected through advertising, use the links in section 3; that data is held by Google, not by us.

10. Your Rights

Data-protection law gives you rights of access, correction, deletion and objection. These rights are exercised against whoever holds your data. We hold none, so there is nothing to request from us. For data collected by Google's advertising services, contact Google using the links in section 3; for iCloud and Game Center, contact Apple.

11. Changes to This Policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, we update this page and change the date at the top. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy, or anything else: yebappcontact@gmail.com. We read every message.
