2. Words and Language

This one deserves its own section, because it is the thing people write to us about most.

OneLet builds its puzzles from open, licensed dictionaries, filtered by hand for each language. Two things follow from that, and both are normal:

  • A word you know may not be in the game. No word list contains every word, and rare or regional words are often missing.
  • A word you do not know may appear in the game. Dictionaries contain words that most speakers never use.

We curate the lists continuously and we remove entries that native speakers tell us are not real words. A word being accepted or rejected by the game is a statement about our word list, not a statement about the language, and never a judgement about you. If something looks wrong, please write to us — those messages are how the lists get better.

Offensive words are excluded from the game entirely, and sensitive words — religious, medical, or violent terms that also have ordinary meanings — are never used as a puzzle's first or last word. If something slipped through, tell us and it will be removed.
