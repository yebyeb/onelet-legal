Support & Help

Last updated: 28 August 2026

How the game works

You start with one word and have to reach another. Hold a letter at the bottom and drop it onto a letter in your word to swap it.

  • Only one letter changes at a time.
  • Every step must be a real word — you cannot pass through something that is not in the dictionary.
  • The empty rows above your word are your remaining moves. There is no separate counter: the board is the counter.

"That is a real word — why was it rejected?"

This is the question we get most, and often you are right.

The game uses open dictionaries filtered by hand. No word list is complete, so real words do go missing — especially rare, regional or technical ones. When a speaker tells us a word is missing, we add it.

Please write to us with the exact word. That single message is the fastest way it gets fixed, and it fixes it for everyone.

"This is not a word — why did the game accept it?"

The reverse happens too: dictionaries contain entries most people never use. Tell us which word and it will be removed. A word being in the game is a statement about our list, not about the language.

I am stuck on a level

You cannot get permanently stuck. Every level is built so that any move can be undone — the keyboard always contains the letters you need to walk back. If you run out of moves, the ladder simply breaks and the level restarts. Nothing is lost, there are no lives, and there is no waiting.

If you want help, the tools above the board offer it: one narrows the keyboard, one points at the tile that changes, one reveals a letter, one plays a whole step. They cost light, which you earn by playing.

There is no sound

  • Check the silent switch on the side of your iPhone. The game plays through it by design, but if the volume is down you will still hear nothing.
  • Check Settings → Sound inside the game.
  • If a call or another app interrupted the game, leaving and re-entering the puzzle restores audio.

I lost my progress

Progress is stored on your device only — we have no server and no copy. If you deleted the app or reset the device, the progress is gone and we cannot restore it. We are sorry; that is the cost of not collecting your data.

Your ad-free purchase is the exception: Apple records it. Use Settings → Restore Purchases.

I paid but still see ads

Open Settings → Restore Purchases. If it still does not work, write to us with the Apple ID email used for the purchase and we will help you chase it with Apple. Do not buy it a second time.

Ads

OneLet only shows rewarded video, and only when you tap a button asking for it. No ad interrupts a puzzle, and no level requires one. If you would rather have none at all, the ad-free purchase removes them.

Notifications

The game can remind you about the daily puzzle. These are scheduled by your own device — nothing is sent from a server. Turn them off any time in iOS Settings, or decline the request when the game asks.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete my data erases progress, light, streak, notebook and settings immediately and permanently. The ad-free purchase record is deliberately kept, so paying once is enough.

Still stuck? Write to us. Tell us your device, the iOS version, and — if it is about a word — the exact word and the level. We read every message.
